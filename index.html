Your attack surface is bigger than your application.

Security effort tends to go where the engineering effort goes: into the application. An attacker looks at six layers and uses whichever one gives way first.

  • A phishing email handing over credentials.

  • An injection flaw or broken access control.

  • An endpoint returning another user’s data.

  • A leaked access key or an account without MFA.

  • An exposed admin port or an unpatched laptop.

  • A public storage bucket or an over-permissive IAM role.

One weak layer is enough. See how we cover all six→
