Meet ThreatReady.

A cybersecurity job-readiness platform that helps candidates find out where they stand before the interview.

  • ThreatReadyCybersecurity job-readiness platform

Cybersecurity job-readiness platform

ThreatReady

Find out whether you can do the security job before the interview does.

The problem
A certificate shows what someone studied, not what they can do when an alert fires. Most candidates only discover the gap in the interview room, and most employers after the hire.
What it does
A cybersecurity readiness platform that runs AI interviews built from a candidate’s resume and the target job description, puts them through role-specific attack simulations and scores both against one skill profile.
Why it matters
Candidates walk in knowing where they stand for the role and what to fix first.
